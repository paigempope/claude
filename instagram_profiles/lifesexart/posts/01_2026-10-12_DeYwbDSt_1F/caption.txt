Ryuichi Sakamoto — Opus (2023)

Knowing that his time was drawing to an end, Ryuichi Sakamoto, legendary Japanese composer who gave many films their musical soul, sat down at his piano for one last performance.
No audience. No spectacle. Just a man, his piano, and a lifetime of devotion. A deeply intimate testament to the beauty and fragility of being human, and to the extraordinary power of art to touch the soul.
