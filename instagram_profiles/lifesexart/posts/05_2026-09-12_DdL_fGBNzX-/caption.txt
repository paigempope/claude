a famous quote by the 13th-century Persian poet and Sufi mystic Rumi.

Recognition, not discovery:  it suggest that love can be a deep and sudden recognition of a soul connection that already exists before two people physically meet.

Union as a reunion: In Sufi mysticism, it is believed that lovers share the same divine essence. Meeting someone you love in the physical world is just a reflection of an eternal bond your souls share beyond time. 

Wholeness: You're not looking for another person to complete you. Instead, you approach a relationship from a place of inner wholeness, seeing your partner as a mirror for your own spiritual growth.
