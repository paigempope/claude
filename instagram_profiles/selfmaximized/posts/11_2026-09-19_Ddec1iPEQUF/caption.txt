REST IN PEACE, MY PERFECT BLUEPRINT:  My father was pure love. He was kindness made human. Warmth made flesh. A man who found profound beauty in the simplest things: a perfectly cut plate of fruit, the sound of birds in the morning, a clean line drawn on paper. He was an engineer who saw the world as something to be understood, organized, and cherished. He drew blueprints the way other people wrote poetry. With precision. With care. Unhurried. With a belief that things done well are a form of love.

So much of Self Maximized is inspired by him. The belief that the most special things in life don’t need to loudly announce themselves. They live in the quiet and the simple. That intentionality matters. That connection is everything. That understated humbleness is what I have always tried to bring to this space. He was the original blueprint for it.

He was also my blueprint for love. The way he loved my mother, steadily and without condition for thirty eight years, showed me exactly what to look for. I am so blessed I found it and he was here to witness it.

A year ago we received the words no family is ever ready to hear. Stage 4 cancer. Terminal. That heartache does not arrive all at once. It comes in waves, in the contradicting space between the delusional hope and the harsh reality that we learned to inhabit. There is nothing more heartbreaking than watching someone you love suffer knowing no amount of love is enough to take it away. 

I watched him fight for over a year with a grace that humbled everyone around him. The night before he took his last breath I held his hand the entire night. It was my way of making sure he never felt alone. My shoulder still sore days later because I refused to let go.

Grief is just love with nowhere to go. To grieve deeply is to have loved deeply. And I loved him with everything I had. Now I am learning to carry both things at once. The devastation and the gratitude. Grief and love are not opposites. They are the same song, sung at the same time. The ability to suffer and sing at the same time.

You were my perfect blueprint. We’ll meet again, in everything you and I loved. 
1954-2026 🕊️
