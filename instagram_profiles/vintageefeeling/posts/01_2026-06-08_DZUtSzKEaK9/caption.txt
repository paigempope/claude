I think one of the rarest things in this world is finding someone who lets you exist exactly as you are.
No performances, no careful versions of yourself, no need to shrink your emotions or hide the strange little things that make you you.
Just two souls meeting softly in the middle of all the chaos, saying:
‘you don’t have to become someone else for me to love you.’
