There is something beautiful about becoming yourself in a world that rewards imitation.
About choosing softness after pain, honesty after disappointment, and authenticity even when it would be easier to wear a mask.
Not everyone will understand your heart, your silence, your way of feeling so deeply but you were never created to fit perfectly into everyone else’s expectations.
You were created to grow, to change, to heal, and to bloom into someone real.
And maybe that is the bravest thing a person can do: to remain true to themselves while the world keeps asking them to become someone else #vintageefeeling ⚡️
