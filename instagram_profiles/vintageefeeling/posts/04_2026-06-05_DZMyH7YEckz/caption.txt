There is nothing selfish about becoming your own person. The problem begins when people stop thinking individually and only think as part of a collective that fears difference, boundaries, and freedom. That is how control becomes normalized and emotional invasion gets disguised as love, protection, or concern.

People start carrying problems that are not theirs, interfering in lives and relationships that do not belong to them, forgetting that every person has the right to choose who they are and how they want to live. Love without respect for individuality will never be healthy.

Real connections are not defined by blood, obligation, or forced closeness, but by respect, trust, and freedom. Because no one should be pressured into becoming someone they are not just to keep others comfortable.

And even when someone chooses to remain part of that collective mindset, there is often an inner disturbance that quietly remains. Because human beings are not naturally made to silence themselves forever. Individuality is not rebellion. It is something natural, healthy, and deeply human #vintageefeeling ⚡️
