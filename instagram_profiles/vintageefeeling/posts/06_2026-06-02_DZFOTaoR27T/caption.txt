Many people criticize or think that mental and emotional health is just an excuse to justify being broken or acting a certain way, but having a healthy mind and emotions that know how to regulate themselves is one of the most important things in life. We live in a world where some people destroy what is fragile like glass, and when it breaks, it leaves cracks, wounds, scars. But not everything is lost. You have to work deeply on your self-esteem, your self-love, your dignity, because in order to truly love others, you first have to love yourself. And that is not simply selfishness. It can become selfishness if you stay trapped in yourself, but real healing means learning how to step outside of yourself and give something good to others too. Still, you cannot truly give if you are empty inside. To give love, peace, patience, or kindness, you need to be okay with yourself first. You need to value yourself, set boundaries, learn how to say no, stop self-destroying, stop torturing yourself, and stop speaking to yourself with cruelty #vintageefeeling ⚡️
