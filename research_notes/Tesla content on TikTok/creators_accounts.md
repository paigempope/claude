# Tesla-Focused Creators and Accounts on TikTok (as of Oct 2026)

Method note: TikTok profile and video pages could not be fetched directly (WebFetch returned an empty "TikTok - Make Your Day" shell). Follower counts below come from search-engine snapshots of TikTok profile pages, Wikipedia, and third-party rankings, and are approximate. Several important pages (Fast Company, SCMP) returned 403. Treat every count as a point-in-time snapshot of uncertain date unless a date is given.

## 1. Who are the largest Tesla creators on TikTok (follower counts, avg views) and what niches do they occupy?

### Takeaway
There is no public Tesla-specific TikTok ranking. The largest Tesla-dedicated accounts I could verify are mid-sized, at about 0.3M to 3M followers: @tesla.flex at about 3M, @the_golden_tesla at about 2.1M, @teslaownerssv at about 923K and @tesla.tok at about 329K. They are much smaller than general car or celebrity creators who make Tesla videos occasionally, such as Daniel Mac (about 14.2M) and Supercar Blondie (20.4M). Tesla reach on TikTok is spread across many small owner accounts plus big generalist creators. A few big owner channels do not dominate it.

## 2. Does Tesla run an official TikTok? How does Tesla's no-ads/owner-marketing approach shape the ecosystem?

### Takeaway
I found no evidence of an active official, verified US Tesla brand account on TikTok. Searches for an "official Tesla TikTok launch" returned nothing. Musk himself joined TikTok as @elonmusk around April 2026, with a single corporate-style sizzle reel. Tesla's traditional near-zero ad spend leaves the TikTok space to owners, generalist creators and critics. Tesla began some paid social advertising and creator sponsorships in 2024–2026, but the creator deals traced here were all on Instagram, not TikTok.

### Cited Findings
- **Musk's own account:** Musk's verified @elonmusk TikTok debuted with one video, an upbeat reel for Neuralink, Tesla, The Boring Company and SpaceX. X was absent from it. The caption was "First TikTok. Ad astra 🚀" and the article is dated April 13, 2026. It does not mention any Tesla TikTok account — [PR Daily](https://www.prdaily.com/the-scoop-elon-musk-strikes-a-hopeful-tone-with-new-tiktok-account/)
- **Musk's early follower count:** Per iFeng, citing the NYT, Musk's account had about 104.6K followers at its first post and nearly 120K within a week — [iFeng](https://tech.ifeng.com/c/8sHefjyeou8)
- **Musk's shifting stance:** SCMP describes Musk's move from calling TikTok something for "probing minds" to having a verified account (page blocked; title only) — [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3350003/probing-minds-verified-account-how-elon-musks-stance-tiktok-shifted)
- **Tesla on other platforms (2023, older):** A 2023 Not a Tesla App piece on Tesla's social-media footprint lists official accounts on other platforms but not TikTok — [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/1375/tesla-s-social-media-domination-)
- **Ad spend on X:** Tesla spent $400,000 on X advertising in 2024 and only $10,000 in the first two months of 2025. It bought ads on X, Reddit, Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Search to promote Musk's pay package, not cars — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/tesla-doesnt-usually-advertise-cars-093156057.html)
- **Social ads before the 2025 shareholder meeting:** Tesla began running social-media ads ahead of the Nov 6, 2025 shareholder meeting. Its marketing otherwise relies on word of mouth and Musk's media presence — [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/2036299/reason-why-never-seen-tesla-commercial-tv/)
- **2022 ad spend (unverified):** Secondary blogs often cite Tesla's 2022 US ad spend at about $152K, against Ford's $370M and Toyota's $1.1B. No primary source is given, so treat it with caution — [Brandvm](https://www.brandvm.com/post/teslas-marketing-strategy)
- **Sponsored creators (2026):** Modash tracks Tesla-sponsored creator content and says 33 creators have worked with Tesla. Its visible examples are all Instagram, from Sep 2026:
  - @ambersharniece: about 57.9K views
  - @thehotceo: "Tesla sent me a car for the night", about 5.8K views
  - @aieshabeasley: Model Y in Arizona, about 13.5K views

  Modash estimates 50% of the deals are with micro creators (10K–100K) and 33% with mid-tier creators (100K–500K). These are single-vendor estimates — [Modash influencers](https://www.modash.io/content-library/brands/tesla-examples/influencers); [Modash examples](https://www.modash.io/content-library/brands/tesla-examples)
- **Legacy automakers are present:** Mercedes-Benz (4.4M), Toyota (4.4M), Lotus (2.7M), Renault (1.7M) and Dodge (998K) all run TikTok accounts in the cars top 50 — [Buzzlytics](https://www.buzzlytics.io/top/tiktok/cars)

### Inferences
- **Tesla's absence on TikTok:** With no visible official account, the "#tesla" feed is defined by owners, hype aggregators, competitor dealers and critics. Rivals such as Toyota dealers can therefore set the narrative (see Q3).
- **Platform split:** Tesla's paid creator activity appears to be micro-influencer lifestyle seeding on Instagram, possibly the "loaner car for the night" format. TikTok seeding is unconfirmed. Tesla's own messaging still runs mainly through X and Musk.
- **Why sentiment skews negative:** TikTok's audience skews younger and more anti-Musk, while X skews pro-Musk (see Q3). With no official presence to counter this, sentiment toward Tesla on TikTok skews more critical than on X.

### Gaps
- **No authoritative confirmation:** Neither Tesla nor TikTok has confirmed that Tesla has no official TikTok. Regional Tesla accounts, such as Tesla China on Douyin, were not verified.
- **Tesla spend on TikTok:** No data was found on any Tesla ad spend or creator spend on TikTok specifically.

## 3. Which specific Tesla videos went massively viral (10M+ views) in 2024–2026, and why?

### Takeaway
Two kinds of Tesla content are documented at multi-million to tens-of-millions scale, both of them critical or comedic rather than owner-promotional. One is Toyota of Hattiesburg's Cybertruck-vs-Sequoia "MomTok" parody from Sept 2024, which had "tens of millions" of views across platforms. The other is the anti-Musk "Cybertruck Hunters" projector pranks and the #ByeTesla trade-in trend from Feb–Mar 2025, with "millions of views". I could not verify any single pro-Tesla TikTok at 10M+ with a specific count.

### Cited Findings
- **Toyota of Hattiesburg, Mississippi (Sept 2024):** The dealership posted a deadpan "MomTok"-style TikTok comparing the Cybertruck with a Toyota Sequoia as a family vehicle. It covered cargo, child-seat access, third row and hidden storage, mocking Tesla owners who make ordinary features seem revolutionary.
  - Views: "tens of millions" across TikTok, X and Facebook.
  - The social media manager said "almost all" TikTok comments were anti-Cybertruck, while X responses were very different. He attributed the gap to Musk owning X.

  [Yahoo Autos](https://autos.yahoo.com/toyota-dealerships-viral-tiktok-roasts-164200893.html)
- **Cybertruck Hunters (early 2025):** This TikTok account projected anti-Musk messages onto Cybertruck tailgates at night, including the inauguration salute and "Most Recalled Truck in 2024". It went viral within about a month. Rachel Maddow featured it on MSNBC on Feb 21, 2025, saying some videos "have millions of views" — [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/tiktok-hunting-tesla-cybertrucks-project-anti-musk-message-tailgate-back/); [Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/2025/02/tiktokers-are-hunting-cybertrucks-with-anti-elon-musk-messages/); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91303178/tesla-cybertrucks-are-getting-roasted-on-tiktok-one-prank-at-a-time)
- **#ByeTesla (2025):** Owners posted Tesla trade-ins set to Taylor Swift's "Look What You Made Me Do". Fast Company quoted the line "Imagine having Cybertruck money and buying a Cybertruck" — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91309612/tiktok-is-full-of-people-trading-in-their-teslas-to-the-sound-of-taylor-swift); [Wikipedia: Tesla Takedown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla_Takedown)
- **Robotaxi/Cybercab clips:** These spread widely on TikTok after the Oct 2024 We, Robot event and the June 2025 Austin launch. TikTok has a "Tesla Robotaxi Clips Go Viral in Austin" trending page. Sampled clips were in the hundreds of thousands, for example about 420.7K plays (a Paris Match Cybercab clip) and about 222.7K. A Spanish-language driverless-Tesla joke clip reached about 811.7K likes. These figures come from search snippets and the metric type is uncertain — [TikTok trending](https://www.tiktok.com/en/trending/detail/tesla-robotaxi-clips-go-viral-in-austin); [The Times on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@thetimes/video/7424844859187399968)
- **Robotaxi safety driver (San Francisco):** A clip of a robotaxi safety driver appearing to nod off went viral on Reddit with 11K+ upvotes, prompting regulators to follow up — [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/viral-video-shows-tesla-robotaxi-202039760.html)
- **Cybertruck Philippines tag page:** Shows clips with 1.8M and 2.3M likes, which implies tens of millions of views, but they are undated — [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/cybertruck-philippines)
- **Older viral fakes (pre-2024):** Ricco Kimborough's viral "defective Model S airbag" TikTok was debunked, and he apologized because the car was not his — [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-model-s-airbag-defect-tiktok-debunked-video/); [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/517105/fake-defective-tesla-video-apology/)

### Inferences
- **Why the biggest videos spread:** They combine (a) the Cybertruck's polarizing look, (b) Musk's political controversy in 2025, and (c) comedy formats native to TikTok, such as deadpan parody, pranks and trending sounds. TikTok's anti-Musk-leaning audience amplifies critical content, while pro-Tesla virality concentrates on X.
- **Event-driven spikes:** Product events such as Robotaxi/Cybercab and FSD versions create short spikes for hype accounts, but documented views stay in the sub-1M range.

### Gaps
- **Exact view counts:** None were obtained for any 10M+ Tesla TikTok, and none for the Hattiesburg video on TikTok alone.
- **2026 TikTok-native mega-virals:** None were found. The Feb 2026 FSD lake clip went viral on X.

## 4. How do these creators monetize (affiliate/referral codes, accessories, Shop links, TikTok Shop)?

### Takeaway
Monetization is mainly through accessory affiliate and TikTok Shop sales, plus paid partnerships with aftermarket brands. Tesla referral codes still exist but pay less than before, about $250 in credits per referral capped at 10 a year in the US. TikTok's Creator Rewards Program pays only on original videos over 1 minute, which is a poor fit for short Tesla clips.

### Cited Findings
- **TikTok Shop accessories:** TikTok Shop has dedicated Tesla accessory search pages: "best tesla model y accessories", "tesla bling car accessories", "tesla girl accessories" and "RPM Tesla accessories". Products include sunshades (one listing at $56.99 with 136 sold), vacuum phone mounts and USB docking hubs — [TikTok Shop: best Model Y accessories](https://shop.tiktok.com/us/k/best-tesla-model-y-accessories); [Tesla bling](https://shop.tiktok.com/us/k/tesla-bling-car-accessories); [Tesla girl](https://shop.tiktok.com/us/k/tesla-girl-accessories); [RPM](https://shop.tiktok.com/us/k/rpm-tesla-accessories)
- **Paid partnerships:** Accessory videos from Tesla creators, such as @teslatainment's, are marked as paid partnerships with aftermarket brands — [TikTok @teslatainment](https://www.tiktok.com/@teslatainment/video/7278135657158004011)
- **Aftermarket brands:** Yeslak claims "200,000+ owners". DIY Wrap Club and Teslahubs market best sellers such as screen protectors, noise-reduction kits and CarPlay units. These are self-reported claims — [Yeslak](https://www.yeslak.com/); [DIY Wrap Club](https://www.diywrapclub.com/collections/tesla-accessories-best-sellers); [Teslahubs](https://teslahubs.com/collections/model-y)
- **Tesla referral program (2026):** Referrers earn $250 in Tesla Credits per referral, up to 10 a year, cut from $500. Buyers get 3 months of FSD (Model 3/Y) or $1,000 off S/X/Cybertruck. Terms vary by market and date — [Rivo](https://www.rivo.io/blog/tesla-referral-program-complete-breakdown); [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Tesla-offers-free-FSD-instead-of-cash-credit-for-referrals-but-keeps-the-loyalty-discounts.997945.0.html)
- **Referral history:** In Sept 2025 Tesla offered bigger rewards, such as $1,000 off for the first 10 S/X/Cybertruck referrals — [Tesery](https://www.tesery.com/blogs/news/tesla-bolsters-referral-program-with-big-change-and-bigger-rewards). An earlier program, which offered merchandise, software and FSD credits, was ended — [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/1996/un)
- **Creator Rewards Program:** Pays about $0.50–$2.00+ per 1,000 qualified views, only on original videos over 1 minute. This comes from a vendor guide — [InfluenceFlow](https://influenceflow.io/resources/tiktok-creator-payment-breakdown-guide-complete-2026-edition/)
- **Tesla's own sponsorships:** Tesla's paid creator seeding exists, but in the traced cases it was on Instagram (see Q2) — [Modash](https://www.modash.io/content-library/brands/tesla-examples/influencers)
- **Cybertruck rental income:** Owners have earned it through peer-to-peer rental, about $5K in 2 months for one owner in 2024 — [autoevolution](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/this-influencer-bought-a-cybertruck-and-it-turned-him-against-tesla-233903.html)

### Inferences
- **Accessories are the main revenue line:** The accessory/"must-have mods" format suits TikTok Shop affiliate commissions. This is likely the main revenue source for dedicated Tesla TikTokers, given the capped $250 referral rewards and the short-video payout limits.

### Gaps
- **Creator earnings:** No creator-specific earnings, affiliate rates or link-in-bio partner data were found.
- **Tesla's own TikTok Shop:** No evidence was found that Tesla sells its own merchandise on TikTok Shop.

## 5. Which creators cross-post from YouTube/X and how does content differ on TikTok?

### Takeaway
Cross-posting is common, but the evidence is thin. @teslaownerssv repurposes X-style hype clips. Ryan's Model Y runs YouTube, TikTok and X. Lamar MK's dispute played out on YouTube and X. The clearest documented difference between platforms is audience sentiment: the same Cybertruck content draws hostile comments on TikTok and supportive ones on X.

### Cited Findings
- **@teslaownerssv:** Posts the Robotaxi, Cybercab and FSD clips typical of X Tesla-fan accounts, with heavy TSLA/elonmusk hashtags — [TikTok @teslaownerssv](https://www.tiktok.com/@teslaownerssv)
- **@ryanjaycowan (Ryan's Model Y):** A YouTuber and TikToker with an X presence of about 15.6K followers — [X](https://x.com/ryanjaycowan?lang=en)
- **Lamar MK:** Documented his Cybertruck lemon dispute on YouTube and tagged Tesla executives on X — [autoevolution](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/this-influencer-bought-a-cybertruck-and-it-turned-him-against-tesla-233903.html)
- **Sentiment gap:** Toyota of Hattiesburg's manager said TikTok comments on the same video were almost all anti-Cybertruck while X responses were "very different" — [Yahoo Autos](https://autos.yahoo.com/toyota-dealerships-viral-tiktok-roasts-164200893.html)
- **Cross-platform hits:** The biggest FSD controversy clip of 2026 spread on X (1.2M+ views in hours), and pranks that began on TikTok crossed into cable news (MSNBC) — [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/02/16/tesla-full-self-driving-tried-to-drive-owner-into-lake-viral-video/); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/tiktok-hunting-tesla-cybertrucks-project-anti-musk-message-tailgate-back/)

### Inferences
- **How content differs by platform:** On TikTok, Tesla content leans toward short "hidden feature" and accessory hacks, lifestyle content, comedy and critique. On X, it leans toward stock and FSD-version analysis and Musk-adjacent advocacy. YouTube holds long-form reviews, such as Bjørn Nyland's and JerryRigEverything's Cybertruck content ([Wikipedia: Bjørn Nyland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bj%C3%B8rn_Nyland_(YouTuber)); [Wikipedia: JerryRigEverything](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JerryRigEverything)). Whether these YouTubers cross-post to TikTok was not verified.

### Gaps
- **Unconfirmed TikTok presence of big names:** Could not confirm TikTok accounts or follower counts for the major Tesla YouTubers and X figures: Whole Mars Catalog, Sawyer Merritt, Dirty Tesla, Chuck Cook, Out of Spec, Kim Java, Black Tesla, Detroit Borg and others.
- **Same video across platforms:** No quantitative comparison of the same creator's video across platforms was found.
