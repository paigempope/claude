How it works

    
Someone needs a part. You know where to get it. Every request runs the same loop:

    
    
      
  1. New — get exact part #, revision, qty, needed-by date, ship-to. Log it the same day.
    2. 
      
  2. Sourcing — call 1–3 suppliers; enter unit cost and shipping.
    3. 
      
  3. Quoted — use the suggested price (or override). Send a written quote valid for the days in Settings.
    4. 
      
  4. Won — customer says yes in writing. Collect payment or a deposit covering your full cost.
    5. 
      
  5. Ordered — buy from the supplier; drop-ship direct to the customer.
    6. 
      
  6. Shipped — send tracking; confirm it's the right part.
    7. 
      
  7. Paid — collect the balance and enter Date Paid. Profit lands on the Dashboard.
    8. 
    

    
The math

    
landed = qty × unit cost + shipping/fees

       quote = roundUp( max(landed × (1 + tier%), landed + minProfit) ÷ (1 − card fee%) )

       profit = price × (1 − card fee%) − landed,   margin = profit ÷ price

    
Markup is profit ÷ cost; margin is profit ÷ price. 25% markup = 20% margin.

    
Rules

    
    
      
  • Money first. Never pay a supplier before the customer has paid at least your landed cost.
    • 
      
  • In writing. Every quote and every yes, by text or email.
    • 
      
  • Exact match. Confirm part #, revision and spec before ordering.
    • 
      
  • Mirror the supplier's returns policy on your quote.
    • 
      
  • Protect your sources. Drop-ship with a blind packing slip.
    • 
    

    
Margins under 10% show in red. Requests past their follow-up date show in orange on the Dashboard.
