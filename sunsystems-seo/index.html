Answers

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy tanning bed parts and red light bed parts?

Right here. We source tanning bed parts, red light bed parts and tanning salon equipment supplies by request for every make and model, including discontinued beds: lamps, ballasts, starters, acrylics, timers, fans, gas springs, electrical parts, LED panels and drivers. Shipped anywhere in the US. See tanning bed parts, red light bed parts and salon equipment supplies.

What tanning bed brands do you repair?

All major makes and models, including Ergoline, ETS, KBL, Wolff, ProSun, SunStar, Montego Bay, Sunmaster, and Sun Dash. If you run a bed or booth in your salon, we can service it.

Do you come to my salon or do I ship the equipment?

We provide on-site service throughout Metro Detroit. A technician comes to your salon, diagnoses the problem, and repairs the equipment in place so your beds get back online fast.

Can you get parts for older or discontinued tanning beds?

Yes. We supply tanning parts by request, including ballasts, starters, timers, fans, acrylics, and lamps. Tell us your bed model and the part you need and we will source it.

Do you sell used tanning beds?

Yes. We sell quality used tanning beds, stand-ups, and booths at a fraction of new prices. Every unit is inspected and serviced by our own technicians before sale, and we can deliver and install it in your salon. Call (248) 545-5577 to hear what is available.

Can you help me open a new tanning salon?

Yes. We help with new salon openings from equipment selection and layout to delivery, installation, wiring, ventilation, and startup so your salon opens ready to run.

Do you install tanning beds or red light beds in homes?

Yes. We install tanning beds, red light therapy beds and panels in private homes, home gyms and wellness rooms across Metro Detroit and Michigan. We handle delivery, assembly, electrical coordination, ventilation and startup, and we can help you choose the right unit before you buy. See our installation, red light bed installation and red light equipment pages.

How fast can you fix a broken tanning bed?

Most common failures such as ballasts, starters, timers, and fans are diagnosed and repaired in a single visit. Call (248) 545-5577 to schedule and we will prioritize equipment that is down and costing you sessions.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the greater Metro Detroit area and Michigan, including Royal Oak, Ferndale, Southfield, Troy, Warren, Sterling Heights, and surrounding communities. We come to your salon. Parts ship anywhere in the US.
