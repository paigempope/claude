Why we do not run a catalog

A tanning bed is a 20 to 40 year machine built from parts that changed every few model years. The same bed name can hide three different acrylic sizes, two ballast types and a timer that was swapped mid-production. Online catalogs handle that with a compatibility chart and a return policy. We handle it by looking at your bed first. Hundreds of Michigan salons have used us this way since 1990 because the part that shows up is the part that fits.

Find the model plate

Every bed has a plate with the manufacturer, model, serial number and electrical rating. On lay-down beds, check the end cap of the base, the inside wall of the base near the lamp ends, or the rear edge of the canopy. Stand-up booths usually carry it inside the door frame or low on the back panel. If the plate is gone, a photo of the whole bed, the timer and a lamp end label gets us most of the way there.

Read the lamp code

Each lamp has a code printed near one end. It tells the length and diameter (for example F71 or F73 in a T12 tube), the wattage (commonly 100 watt or 160 watt), and the end type: bi-pin or recessed double contact. The bed's own lamp requirement is usually on the plate or on a label inside the canopy. Those two codes need to match. If they do not, the bed may light but will not perform, and some mismatches damage the ballast. More on this on the lamps page.

Where can I buy tanning bed parts?

Do you carry red light bed parts?

Yes. LED panels, LED drivers and power supplies, control boards and timers, cooling fans, acrylics and gas springs for red light therapy beds, panels and hybrid tanning beds. See red light bed parts.

Can you get parts for a discontinued tanning bed?

Usually, yes. Lamps, starters, ballasts, gas springs and many electrical parts are standard components that fit a wide range of beds. Acrylics and model-specific boards are harder, but we have relationships built over 35 years and we will tell you straight if a part cannot be had.

How do I find my tanning bed model number?

Look for a metal or foil plate with the manufacturer name, model, serial number, voltage and amperage. On most lay-down beds it is on the end of the base, inside the base near the lamps, or on the back edge of the canopy. Stand-up booths usually have it inside the door frame or on the back panel. Have a photo of it ready when you call, or attach it to your reply to our confirmation email.

Will you install the part?

In Michigan, yes. We come to the salon or the home, install it and test the bed at full output. Outside Michigan we ship the part and walk you through the install on a paid video session.

What do I need to send with a parts request?

Your name, phone, city and state, the make and model, and the part you need. A photo of the model plate and a photo of the failed part are the two things that remove guesswork. Reply to our confirmation email with the photo.
