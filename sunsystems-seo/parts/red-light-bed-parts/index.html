Red light bed parts we source

  • LED panels and arrays. Replacement panels for sections that are dark, dim or showing dead diodes.
  • LED drivers and power supplies. The most common reason one section of a red light bed goes dark. We test the driver before anyone buys a panel.
  • Control boards and timers. Main boards, touch controls, session timers and remote timer systems.
  • Cooling fans. Red light beds run warm and shut down when cooling fails. Fans, fan controllers and thermal cutoffs.
  • Acrylics and covers. Bench and canopy acrylics for cracked, crazed or hazed beds. See acrylics.
  • Gas springs and canopy hardware. For lids that drift down or will not stay open. See gas springs.
  • Wiring, connectors and electrical. Harnesses, connectors, relays and cords. See electrical.

Hybrid tanning bed parts

A hybrid bed is a tanning bed with red light built in, so it needs parts from both worlds. We source UV tanning lamps and red light lamps, ballasts, starters, LED panels and drivers, timers and fans, and relamp hybrids as a matched set.

Common red light bed problems and the part behind them

  • One panel or row is dark: driver or power supply first, then the connector, then the panel.
  • Whole bed dead: breaker, cord, main relay or control board. See bed won't turn on.
  • Shuts off mid-session: cooling fan or thermal cutoff.
  • Trips the breaker: circuit size or a shorted component. See bed keeps tripping the breaker.
  • Output seems low: aging panels, dirty acrylic or a failing driver. See red light bed irradiance explained.

Can a red light bed be repaired instead of replaced?

Usually, yes. Most failures are a single dead LED panel, a failed driver or power supply, a cooling fan, or a control or timer fault. Replacing the part is almost always far cheaper than replacing the bed.

Why is one section of my red light bed dark?

Usually the LED driver or power supply feeding that section, a loose connector, or the panel itself. We test the driver output before replacing a panel, because the driver is the cheaper and more common failure.

Do you carry parts for hybrid tanning beds with red light?

Yes. We source both sides: UV lamps, red light lamps, ballasts, starters, LED panels, drivers, timers, fans and acrylics.
