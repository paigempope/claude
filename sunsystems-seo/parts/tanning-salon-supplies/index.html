The short list to keep on the shelf

A few spare starters for every bed that uses them, a few spare lamps of each code you run, a spare fan for your busiest bed, and gas springs for any canopy that has started to drift. That covers most of the failures that take a bed down on a Saturday. Not sure which codes your beds use? Send us the make and model of each bed and we will put the list together.

Frequently asked questions

Where can a tanning salon buy equipment parts and supplies?

Sun Systems and Service in Metro Detroit supplies tanning salon equipment parts by request, for every make including discontinued beds. Michigan salons get the part installed. Salons anywhere in the US get it shipped after a video session.

What parts should a tanning salon keep on hand?

Starters for every bed that uses them, a few spare lamps of each code you run, a spare fan for your busiest bed, and gas springs for any canopy that is starting to drift.

How often should a salon relamp its tanning beds?

Most salon tanning lamps are rated for roughly 500 to 1,000 hours depending on the lamp. Relamp the full bed as a matched set when the lamps reach their rated hours. See how often to replace tanning bed lamps, or let the Relamp Plan schedule it for you.

Do you supply parts for salons outside Michigan?

Yes. We confirm the part on a video session, ship it, and walk your staff through the install.
